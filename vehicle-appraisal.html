Vehicle Appraisal in Etobicoke

Need a vehicle appraisal in Etobicoke or the Greater Toronto Area? North Auto Sale provides written, signed vehicle appraisals at our Etobicoke location. Whether your insurance company, ServiceOntario, a lawyer, or an estate needs the value of a vehicle documented, we inspect the car in person, research its current market value, and give you a professional appraisal you can submit with confidence.

When You Need a Vehicle Appraisal

  • Insurance — claims, value disputes, and agreed-value or classic-car policies
  • Vehicle transfers & taxes — retail sales tax on private purchases is based on market value; an appraisal documents the real value of the vehicle
  • Family gift transfers — transferring a vehicle to a spouse or family member
  • Estates & divorce — fair, documented vehicle values for settlements
  • Importing a vehicle — bringing a car into Ontario from the US or another province
  • Donations — establishing fair market value for a charitable tax receipt

What Your Appraisal Includes

  • In-person inspection of the vehicle — condition, odometer, options, and any damage
  • Market-value research using current comparable listings and recent sales
  • A written, signed appraisal from a licensed Ontario dealer
  • Most appraisals completed in about 30–45 minutes, same visit

How It Works

  1. Call ahead — book a time at 647-705-0346 (walk-ins welcome when available)
  2. Bring the vehicle to 145 Belfield Rd, Unit 1, Etobicoke — bring the ownership and your driver's licence
  3. We inspect and research the vehicle's condition and market value
  4. You get the written appraisal — signed and ready for your insurer, lawyer, or ServiceOntario

Vehicle Appraisal FAQs

How much does a vehicle appraisal cost?
The fee covers the inspection, market research, and the written document — call us at 647-705-0346 for current pricing.

How long does it take?
About 30 to 45 minutes for most vehicles. In most cases you leave with the appraisal the same visit.

What should I bring?
The vehicle, its ownership, and your driver's licence. Service records and any forms from your insurer or lawyer help us match the appraisal to their requirements.

Do you appraise any vehicle?
Yes — cars, SUVs, trucks, and classic or specialty vehicles of any age.
